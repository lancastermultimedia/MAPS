MAPS - Modular Atonal Percussion Synthesizer
============================================

A drum machine with no drum samples in it. Three synthesis voices driven by a
topographic pattern generator, through a granular processor.


INSTALLING
----------

macOS

  Copy "MAPS.component" into:

      ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/

  and "MAPS.vst3" into:

      ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/

  Then quit Logic (or your DAW) and open it again. Logic caches its plugin
  scan, so it will not see a new Audio Unit until it restarts.

  MAPS is an INSTRUMENT, not an effect. In Logic it is under
  Instrument -> AU Instruments -> Lancaster Multimedia -> MAPS.

Windows

  Copy the whole "MAPS.vst3" FOLDER (not just the file inside it) into:

      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\

  Windows will ask for administrator permission; that folder is shared
  between all your plugin hosts. Then re-scan in your DAW. In REAPER:
  Options -> Preferences -> Plug-ins -> VST -> Re-scan.


IF YOUR SYSTEM OBJECTS

  The plugin is not code-signed, so macOS Gatekeeper or Windows SmartScreen
  may say it is from an unidentified developer. It is a plain plugin, not an
  installer. On macOS: right-click the file, choose Open, then Open again.


PLAYING IT

  It runs on its own. Put it on an instrument track, press play, and the
  sequencer starts. If the transport is stopped it free-runs at whatever the
  CLOCK knob says.

  MAP X and MAP Y walk through the pattern space. CHAOS loosens it. The three
  DENSITY knobs on the diagonal decide how busy each of the three voices is.

  It also takes MIDI notes alongside the sequencer: 36 is voice one, 38 is
  voice two, 42 is voice three - the usual kick / snare / hat notes - so an
  existing drum part in your timeline will play it without remapping.

  MODULATION: the bar along the bottom of the panel opens three modulation
  sources. Each one has a waveform, a rate, and two routes to any knob on the
  instrument. They lock to your host's tempo by default, so a RANDOM source
  pointed at a voice's MODEL knob picks a different drum engine every beat.
  The presets "Shuffled Engines" and "Engine Ramp" show what that sounds like.

  ONE THING WORTH KNOWING: the DENSITY knob in the granular section (bottom
  right) has a dead spot low down in its travel where nothing comes out. That
  is how the original module behaves and it is not a fault. If the granular
  side goes quiet, that is the knob to check.


WHO MADE WHAT

  The three synthesis engines inside MAPS - Plaits, Clouds and Grids - were
  designed by Emilie Gillet of Mutable Instruments, and released by her under
  the MIT licence. MAPS rearranges them into one instrument. The interesting
  parts are hers.
